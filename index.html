Before you ask
Common questions

      

        
Which platform should I use?
If you already have a site, I'll usually work on the platform you're on. Starting fresh, I'll recommend one based on your budget and how much you want to edit yourself. Wix is a great all-rounder, Shopify suits online stores, and WordPress suits content-heavy sites.

        
How long does a website take?
Fixes and updates often take 1–3 days. A redesign or a new small business website usually takes 1–3 weeks, depending on the number of pages and how ready your content is.

        
Can I update the website myself afterwards?
Yes. Every project ends with a simple walkthrough so you can change text, photos and prices on your own. Ongoing maintenance is available if you'd rather not.

        
Will you help my business show up on Google?
Yes. On-page SEO is part of every build: page titles, descriptions, headings, image alt text and Google Search Console. Local SEO and AI search optimization are available too.
